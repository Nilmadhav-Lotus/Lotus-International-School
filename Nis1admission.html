Admissions

Lotus International School welcomes students from diverse backgrounds and provides a nurturing and inclusive environment for academic and personal growth.

We offer both Online and Offline admission modes for the convenience of parents and guardians.

🌐 Online Admission

  • Fill out the online registration form.
  • Upload scanned copies of required documents.
  • Wait for confirmation call from the admissions team.
  • Attend virtual counseling session (if required).
  • Pay fees online to confirm admission.

📄 Click the button below to start your online registration:

📝 Register Online

🏫 Offline Admission

  • Visit the school reception between 08:00 AM – 03:00 PM (Monday to Saturday).
  • Collect and fill out the physical admission form.
  • Submit the required documents at the front desk.
  • Attend an in-person counseling session and campus tour.
  • Complete admission by submitting fees at the office.
