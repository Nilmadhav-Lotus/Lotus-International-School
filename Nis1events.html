School Events

At Nilmadhav International School, we believe learning goes far beyond the classroom. Our events are crafted to inspire, celebrate, and build a strong community spirit among students and staff. 🌟

📅 Major Events & Celebrations

  • Annual Day Celebration 🎭 – A grand cultural showcase by our talented students.
  • Science & Art Exhibition 🔬🎨 – Creativity meets innovation in this interactive fair.
  • Teacher's Day & Children's Day 🎈 – Honoring those who teach and those who learn!
  • Inter-House Cultural Competitions 🏆 – Music, dance, quiz, drama and more!
  • Field Trips & Educational Tours 🚌 – Learning through travel and exploration.
  • Festival Celebrations 🪔🎄🎉 – Celebrating unity in diversity through major festivals.
  • Sports Day 🏃‍♀️⚽ – Fostering teamwork, fitness, and school spirit.
